Who we are

A REAL WASH BAY, NOT A ROADSIDE BUCKET JOB.

Panther Auto Care runs a paved, canopied wash bay in Agodo Egbe, Ikotun. Cars are washed under cover, interiors vacuumed and detailed on site, and rugs, engines and paint get their own dedicated treatments.

The difference is what you do while you wait. There are full-size snooker tables steps from the bay, free WiFi, and staff who call you the moment your car is ready. Bring work, bring friends, or bring your cue form.

GOT QUESTIONS? WE HAVE ANSWERS.

Real answers about visiting Panther in Ikotun.

Do I need to book in advance?+

No. You can drive straight into the yard at 53 Amudalatu Street. Booking ahead with the form below helps us line up luxury washes, buffing or rug jobs so you wait less.

How long does a wash take?+

A standard wash and vacuum is usually done within the hour, depending on the queue. Luxury washes, buffing and engine steaming take longer. The team will give you a time estimate before they start.

Can I play snooker while my car is washed?+

Yes, that is the point. The tables are steps from the bays. Play as long as your car is being worked on and staff will call you when it is ready for inspection.

Do you wash rugs and interiors?+

Yes. We wash household and car rugs, vacuum seats, carpets and boots, and handle engine steaming and paint buffing as separate services. Mention everything when you book so we plan one visit.

Is there WiFi and parking?+

Yes. Free WiFi is available across the compound and there is paved parking under and around the canopy, with room to queue off the street.

What is the car tracking service?+

We arrange car tracking fittings and setup through the team on site. Send a message with your car model using the booking form and we will call back with options and pricing.
